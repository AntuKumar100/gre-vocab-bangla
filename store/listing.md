## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Learn the vocabulary of the GRE® General Test the way your memory likes it: in Bangla, through stories, a few minutes a day.

Shobdo Quest teaches 1,110 high-value GRE words. Every word comes with:
• a clear English meaning and a Bangla meaning (বাংলা অর্থ)
• a memory trick that makes it stick
• a short story set in everyday Bangladeshi life, narrated in a slow, clear voice
• synonyms and an example sentence
• a real GRE-style question

STUDY SMART, NOT LONG
• 37 sets of 30 words, from the most common to the rarest
• Spaced repetition (FSRS) brings each word back just before you would forget it
• Set your test date and the app plans how many new words to learn each day
• A quick level check at the start tells you where to begin

PRACTICE LIKE THE REAL TEST
• Sentence Equivalence: pick the two words that give the sentence the same meaning
• Text Completion: pick the word that completes the sentence
• After every answer, see the clue in the sentence, why the right words work, and why the trap is wrong
• Optional timer at real GRE pace
• Also: meaning, Bangla, reverse, synonym and spelling questions

STAY MOTIVATED
• XP, levels, daily goals, streaks and combos
• A quest path through all 37 sets
• Daily study reminders

MADE FOR YOU
• Works completely offline, audio included
• Bangla or English interface
• Light and dark themes
• No ads, no account, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone, and you can back it up any time.

Found a mistake? Every word has a "Report a mistake" button.
