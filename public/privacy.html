Privacy policy

Shobdo Quest · effective 27 October 2026

Shobdo Quest does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress, settings and starred words are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. You can export a backup file yourself from Settings; where you save it is your choice.

Network use

The Android app works fully offline. The website version downloads the app and its audio from GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs.

Notifications

Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device. No notification service is involved.

Children

The app is made for students preparing for graduate admission tests and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/AntuKumar100/gre-vocab-bangla/issues.
